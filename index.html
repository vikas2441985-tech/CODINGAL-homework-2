BIOGRAPHY ABOUT

MR. NARENDRA MODI

Narendra Damodardas Modi is an Indian politician who has served as thePrime Minister of India since May 26, 2014. A senior leader of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) and a former volunteer for the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS), he previously served as the Chief Minister of Gujarat from 2001 to 2014. He is the first Prime Minister of India born after the country achieved independence.

📋 Personal Profile • Born: September 17, 1950, in Vadnagar, Gujarat. • Education: BA fromDelhi University (1978) and MA from Gujarat University (1983). • Family: Parents Damodardas Mulchand Modi and Hiraba Modi; married Jashodaben in 1968 before separating. • Constituency: Member of Parliament for Varanasi.

Early Life & Political Rise

Modi grew up helping at his family's tea stall in Vadnagar. Inspired by Swami Vivekananda, he traveled across India as a young man before joining the RSS as a full-time campaigner. He later transitioned to the BJP in 1987, establishing a reputation as an organizer and strategist.

Chief Minister of Gujarat (2001–2014)

Premiership (2014–Present) Modi led the BJP to sweeping parliamentary majorities in 2014 and 2019, securing a third term in 2024 heading a National Democratic Alliance coalition. Major initiatives under his administration include the Goods and Services Tax (GST), demonetisation, the Swachh Bharat Abhiyan, and infrastructure revamps, alongside controversial policy moves such as the revocation of Jammu and Kashmir's special status and the Citizenship Amendment Act.
